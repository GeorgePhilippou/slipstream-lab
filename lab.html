How to use Slipstream
  1. Pick an aircraft. Use the menu at the top left (Light trainer, Sailplane, Airliner…). Everything works on that one aircraft.
  2. Choose a module from the list on the left. The Properties under it are the inputs; the Results on the right update instantly.
  3. Geometry: drag a slider or type in a box (Span, Sweep, Taper…) and the 3D model changes. Drag the model to rotate it; scroll to zoom.
  4. Wing and tail: lift, drag, stall and stability of your design. Use the toolbar to colour the wing by loading or by stall margin, then press Trim with tail incidence.
  5. Airfoil: a live 2D airflow simulation around your wing section. It starts by itself; change the angle of attack, or switch on Supercooled cloud under Icing to grow ice.
  6. Flight and icing: flies your design. Switch on In cloud, or open Scenarios and choose “Hold in icing cloud”, then watch the event log.
  7. 3D flow: 3D airflow on your graphics card. Needs Chrome or Edge 113+, or Safari 26. Drop an STL, OBJ or GLB file on it to test your own model.

The status bar at the bottom shows what the solver is doing. If something fails in your browser, the error appears there too.
